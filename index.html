What Kept is

Kept is a personal media tracker. You log a film, a show, a book or a game once you have finished it, and Kept keeps the record: what you finished, when, what you thought of it, and what you are partway through.

Your record follows your account rather than your phone. Sign in on another device and the same shelves are there.
